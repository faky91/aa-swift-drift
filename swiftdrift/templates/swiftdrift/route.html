  1. Enter start and destination. Both fields autocomplete known k-space systems. The button swaps them for the way back.
  2. Choose your shortcuts. Drifter wormholes connect through their network, normal wormholes are direct connections, and jump bridges are your alliance's Ansiblex gates. Each can be toggled with its checkbox.
  3. Read the route from top to bottom. Each row is one system; the badge shows what you do THERE to move on: Stargate Jump bridge or entering a wormhole. The last row is your Destination.
  4. Enter ... here marks your dive. In that system, warp to the listed bookmark and jump into the wormhole. If the bookmark is unknown, scan or watch for the hole yourself.
  5. Check the status line before you fly. Wormholes collapse. Each wormhole on the route shows its estimated Alive value, size, EOL flag and the pilot reports ( still open / gone). Distrust the route? Use Find alternative route to recalculate without these wormholes.
  6. Set destination guides you to the hole. It sets your in-game destination to the system containing the (first) wormhole on the route. Fly there by autopilot, then continue manually through the hole. On first use, EVE SSO will ask you to authorize the waypoint permission for one of your characters.
