  1. Enter start and destination. Both fields autocomplete known k-space systems.
  2. Choose your shortcuts. "Use drifter wormholes" routes you through reported drifter holes (two holes of the same wormhole type connect through its network). "Use jump bridges" includes your alliance's Ansiblex connections.
  3. Read the route from top to bottom. Each row is one system; the badge shows how you arrive there: Stargate, Jump bridge, or Exit (leaving a wormhole).
  4. "Enter ... here" marks your dive. In that system, warp to the listed bookmark and jump into the wormhole. The exit row shows the bookmark on the far side.
  5. "Set destination" guides you to the hole. It sets your in-game destination to the system containing the (first) wormhole on the route. Fly there by autopilot, then continue manually through the hole. On first use, EVE SSO will ask you to authorize the waypoint permission for one of your characters.
  6. Check before you fly. Wormholes collapse. Verify the Alive value, EOL status and pilot reports of the holes on your route first.
